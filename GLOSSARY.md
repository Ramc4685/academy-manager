# Glossary

One word for each thing in product copy (owner decision D-16, 2026-11-26).
Every new string uses these words; the vocabulary sweep fixes old ones. Code
names (`session`, `occurrence`, `held`, `paused`, `dropped`) stay as they are:
this governs what people read, not identifiers.

**Terminology rule:** say "class" and "class date", never "session" or
"occurrence", to parents, coaches and staff alike. Hold, Pause and Stop are
three different actions; never use one word for another.

## Classes

**Class.** A weekly group a child is enrolled in, such as "Saturday Beginners
9:00". Not "session", "program" or "course". (A program is the curriculum a
class teaches, with its levels and skills.)

**Class date.** One dated meeting of a class, such as "Sat, Dec 5, 9:00".
Not "session", "occurrence" or "lesson". A class date can be cancelled
without changing the class.

## Taking a break or leaving

**Hold.** The child steps away until an agreed return date and keeps their place.
- Seat: kept, up to the academy's hold limit (60 days by default); a full class may reclaim the longest hold if the academy allows it.
- Next bill: none until the return date; billing restarts when they return.

**Pause.** The child steps away with no place kept, usually at the family's request.
- Seat: released; the child joins the waitlist to come back.
- Next bill: none while paused; billing restarts when a seat is free and they resume.

**Stop.** The child leaves a class for good ("Stop all classes" leaves every class).
The current per-class "Drop" button is a Stop and is renamed in the sweep.
- Seat: released from the stop date.
- Next bill: nothing after the stop date, apart from any cancellation fee the academy's policy sets.

## One class date

**Absence.** The family says, before the class date starts, that the child will miss it.
- Seat: kept; the class goes on without them.
- Next bill: unchanged; an absence is not a refund.

**Make-up.** A child who gave notice of an absence attends a different class
date instead, within the academy's make-up window (30 days by default).
- Seat: uses a free place on the make-up date; no seat in the regular class changes.
- Next bill: unchanged; a make-up adds nothing to it.

**Trial.** A free first class date for a child who is not yet enrolled, approved by the academy.
- Seat: uses a free place on that one date only; it holds no place in the class.
- Next bill: none; a trial is never charged.
