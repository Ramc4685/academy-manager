What we heard

The brief

One system that runs a racket-sport academy: schedules, enrollments, attendance, billing, coach payouts and parent messages. The logo has to feel like an academy where players earn their level, energetic and sporty, for any racket sport.

energeticsportyacademyprogress

Success: reads as an academy, fits any racket sport, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, uses the Rally palette.

Concept A

Earned Stripes

Levels earned, stacked like stripes; the newest one is volt.

  • Says academy: progression players earn, the way the app tracks skill levels
  • No racket or ball drawn, so tennis, badminton, pickleball and squash all fit
  • Three strokes on a 3:4 slope: holds at 16 px and in one colour
