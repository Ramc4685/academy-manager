- [contract] README:103 says `pytest v2/tests` with "≥70% coverage on `v2/shared`". CI uses `--cov=v2 --cov-fail-under=86`, which covers all of v2, not just shared (`production.yml:165`).

Cross-context isolation (ADR-0005 rule 5) has no contract either.
- [contract] README:36-40 diagram and README:42 "six layer rules" omit the `composition/` layer. `backend/v2/composition/*.py` is the outermost layer, enforced by the `composition-is-outermost` contract (pyproject.toml:182).
