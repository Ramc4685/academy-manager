# Email campaign audiences, invoice digest counts and waiver invoices

PR: TBD

## What changed
- Email campaign count (#1029): the composer's count comes from the server, not a client estimate. A new `POST /admin/campaigns/preview` resolves the audience the send would reach and emails nobody. Siblings share one family email, so it reads like "7 email recipients on Sat Beginners (7 families + 1 family contact)". The count shows no number while it loads or if it fails.
- Recipient review (#1031): before sending, the admin sees who will get the email and who will not, with the reason: no email address, duplicate address, suppressed (the address is bouncing or complained), or unsubscribed. The send carries the preview's audience fingerprint. If the audience changed in between, the send is refused with 409 `audience_changed` and the admin is asked to review again.
- Several sessions in one send (#1030): an admin can tick several sessions. A family in two of them gets one copy. Single-session campaigns are stored exactly as before; a multi-session one is stored as a new `sessions` audience type.
- Invoice ops digest (#1033): the daily digest now also counts what the send at mint time did: invoices emailed, autopay notices sent, and failed send attempts, folded into the existing digest keys. One new key, `invoice_mint_send_failed`, counts runs where that whole send pass raised. A tick whose only news is a failed send pass now produces a digest instead of staying silent.
- Waiver invoices (#1035): a monthly invoice minted at net $0 over real tuition (a full waiver, or credit covering it) is stamped `zero_net_notice` and emailed by the monthly send pass with "No payment required", never "contact the academy to arrange payment", and never as an autopay "will be charged" notice. An owner can send a hand-billed draft whose charges are fully offset by a waiver; a blank draft is still refused. The invoice email and the autopay notice list every discount, waiver and credit line in its public wording. A coach-child line that covers the whole bill reads "Coach benefit: fees covered by <academy name>"; a partial one keeps its own description. A one-time adjustment's required reason and the acting owner are recorded in the billing audit trail, and a negative adjustment shows on the family timeline as "Discount added to invoice".

## Deploy notes
Deploy backend and frontend together: the composer calls the new preview route and sends `session_ids` plus `expected_audience_fingerprint`. No migration. Only $0 invoices minted after this deploy are emailed: existing waived or credit-covered invoices carry no `zero_net_notice` stamp and are never swept up, so nobody gets a surprise email at deploy.

## Risk / rollback
The preview and the exclusion reasons reuse the sender's own suppression, preference and prospect gates read-only, so nothing a gate blocks can be sent. Waiver delivery adds one branch to the send query (`total_cents` 0 and `zero_net_notice`) and keeps zero balances out of checkout, autopay notices and late fees. To roll back, first drain or delete any stored campaigns with a `sessions` audience: the old code's `parse_audience` cannot read them. Then revert the PR. A leftover `zero_net_notice` field is ignored by the old code.
