# trial-self-booking

## What changed
Families can book a free trial date themselves from the public page. This ships switched off: until the owner turns on "Let families book a trial date", the page and the form behave exactly as before and no new email goes to any family. Once it is on, each published class lists its next three open dates (3 hours to 21 days ahead). A family picks one, passes a Cloudflare Turnstile check and is booked at once. They get a confirmation email with the date, time, place and what to bring, and the owners get a "New trial booking" alert. Each class date takes up to 2 website guests (1 to 5 in settings), never more than the free seats, and two families taking the last seat at once leave exactly one booking. Each inbox can hold one booking per class and two per academy, and an academy takes at most 10 self-bookings a day. A booking that hits a limit quietly stays a normal inquiry. The coach's day lists booked guests by parent name and player age only, with Came / Didn't come. If the academy cancels that date, the guest's request goes back to pending and the guest is emailed. Approving a trial now counts booked guests against the class's seats. While self-booking is on, families also get an email when staff approve (with the date) or decline a trial. The trial form now preselects the class a visitor clicked, asks "How did you hear about us?" and keeps utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign from the landing URL on the contact. The registration parent step stores the same "heard from" answer. These changes are live now, whatever the setting: a family that asks again more than a day after their first inquiry moves the card up (lead to trial, never down), keeps both messages and re-alerts the owners once. An owner alert that never went out (crash or mail outage) is re-sent by a daily sweep.

## Deploy notes
Migration `0236_trial_guest_bookings` adds two partial indexes on `trial_requests`: a unique seat index on (academy, date, guest seat) and a contact lookup index. It applies through the release_command. New settings: backend `V2_TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` (Fly secret) and frontend `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` (GitHub repo variable, read by the production build steps). With no secret the backend skips the Turnstile check, so set it before switching self-booking on. The CSP now allows challenges.cloudflare.com in script-src and frame-src. A new daily job, `resend_due_owner_alerts`, runs at 09:40 academy time.

Owner steps, in this order:
- Create a Cloudflare Turnstile widget for the academy domains, then set `V2_TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` on Fly and `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` as a GitHub repo variable, and redeploy the frontend.
- Read and approve the copy of the four family emails (trial booked, trial confirmed, trial declined, trial date cancelled) in `backend/v2/composition/trial_emails.py`.
- Turn on Settings > Public page > "Let families book a trial date", and set the guests per date if 2 is not right.

## Risk / rollback
Everything family-facing stays off until the setting is turned on, so turning it off again is the fast rollback: the page stops offering dates at once and no new booking or decision email is sent. Bookings already made stay approved on the coach's day. The repeat-inquiry re-alert and the daily owner-alert sweep are live on deploy and only email the academy's owners. Roll back the code by reverting the merge commit. The new indexes can stay, or drop them with `dropIndex("trial_requests_guest_slot_unique")` and `dropIndex("trial_requests_contact")`. Reminders and the no-show follow-up for website guests are not in this change; they come with the next part of the trial funnel work.
