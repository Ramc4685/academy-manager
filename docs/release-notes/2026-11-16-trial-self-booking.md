# trial-self-booking

PR: #1185

## What changed
Families can book a free trial date themselves from the public page. This ships switched off: until the owner turns on "Let families book a trial date", the page and the form behave exactly as before and no new email goes to any family. Once it is on, each published class lists its next three open dates (3 hours to 21 days ahead). A family picks one, passes a Cloudflare Turnstile check (solved on that academy's own page) and is booked at once. They get a confirmation email with the date, time, place and what to bring, and the owners get a "New trial booking" alert. Each class date takes up to 2 website guests (1 to 5 in settings), never more than the free seats, and two families taking the last seat at once leave exactly one booking. Each inbox (address aliases such as name+tag count as one) can self-book one trial per class and hold two upcoming dates at a time per academy (a date stops counting once it starts), and an academy takes at most 10 self-bookings per UTC day; the per-class and daily limits hold even when many bookings arrive at once. A booking that hits a limit quietly stays a lead with a normal inquiry reply. The success screen promises an email if the date is confirmed and a reply within one working day otherwise, so it never claims a booking that did not happen. The coach's day lists booked guests by parent name and player age only, with Came / Didn't come. If the academy cancels that date, the guest's request goes back to pending and the guest is emailed. Staff can also cancel a single guest booking from the trial queue (Cancel booking): the seat and the family's booking limits are freed, the request goes back to pending and the family gets the same date-cancelled email. Approving a trial or a make-up, and the make-up dates offered to parents, now count booked guests against the class's seats. While self-booking is on, families also get an email when staff approve (with the date) or decline a trial. The trial form now preselects the class a visitor clicked, asks "How did you hear about us?" and keeps utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign from the landing URL on the contact. The registration parent step stores the same "heard from" answer. These changes are live now, whatever the setting: a family that asks again more than a day after their first inquiry moves the card up (lead to trial, never down) and re-alerts the owners once. Their original message is never changed: the new note is kept beside it (the last five) and the owner alert marks it as unverified, because anyone can type a family's details into the public form. An owner alert that never went out (crash or mail outage) is re-sent by a daily sweep.

## Deploy notes
Migration `0236_trial_guest_bookings` adds four partial indexes on `trial_requests`: unique indexes on (academy, date, guest seat), (academy, inbox, class) and (academy, daily booking number), and a contact lookup index. It applies through the release_command. New settings: backend `V2_TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` (Fly secret) and frontend `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` (GitHub repo variable, read by the production build steps). In production, with no secret no booking goes through: every submission, including one that picked a date, is filed as a plain inquiry, so set it before switching self-booking on. Dev and CI skip the check. The CSP now allows challenges.cloudflare.com in script-src and frame-src. A new daily job, `resend_due_owner_alerts`, runs at 09:40 academy time.

Owner steps, in this order:
- Create a Cloudflare Turnstile widget for the academy domains, then set `V2_TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` on Fly and `NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` as a GitHub repo variable, and redeploy the frontend.
- Read and approve the copy of the four family emails (trial booked, trial confirmed, trial declined, trial date cancelled) in `backend/v2/composition/trial_emails.py`.
- Turn on Settings > Public page > "Let families book a trial date", and set the guests per date if 2 is not right.

## Risk / rollback
Everything family-facing stays off until the setting is turned on (the trial queue shows Cancel booking only on approved guest bookings, which exist only once it is on), so turning it off again is the fast rollback: the page stops offering dates at once and no new booking or decision email is sent. Bookings already made stay approved on the coach's day. The repeat-inquiry re-alert and the daily owner-alert sweep are live on deploy and only email the academy's owners. Roll back the code by reverting the merge commit. The new indexes can stay, or drop them with `dropIndex` on `trial_requests_guest_slot_unique`, `trial_requests_guest_mailbox_class_unique`, `trial_requests_guest_day_unique` and `trial_requests_contact`. Reminders and the no-show follow-up for website guests are not in this change; they come with the next part of the trial funnel work.
