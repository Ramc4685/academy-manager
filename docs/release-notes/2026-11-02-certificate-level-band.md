# certificate-level-band

PR: #1109

## What changed
The printable level certificate has a cleaner, bolder layout: white paper on the left with the academy logo, "Certificate of achievement", the student's name on a volt marker stroke, the level, a checklist of skills mastered and the date and "Awarded by" lines; on the right, a full-height band in the academy's brand colour with a large volt level number and the shuttle's three feathers, one lit per level reached. The heavy double frame, corner feathers, pill ribbon and round seal are gone. Long names step down in size so they stay on one line, and more than eight skills flow into three columns. Same content, same one-page Letter-landscape print.
