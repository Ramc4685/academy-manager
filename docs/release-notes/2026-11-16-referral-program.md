## What changed
The referral programme from the Family offers card now works, and still ships switched off. The owner can switch it on in Settings > Billing rules once the referring family's credit, the hold days, the yearly cap per referring family and the trigger are set (no default amounts; the new family's credit and the expiry are optional, blank meaning none and never). Switching it on stamps the date it took effect, so only families referred on or after that date count, and each referral keeps the credit, hold and expiry it was recorded with if the owner later changes them (changes are audited as `referral_program_changed`, as before). While it is on, each parent sees a Refer a family card on their profile with a stable 8-character code, a Copy link button (the academy page with `?ref=<code>`) and a count of rewards earned. The website trial form carries `?ref=` into the inquiry's attribution, and a registration can carry `parent_profile.referral_code` (the onboarding form does not send it yet). A daily job (`process_referrals`, 06:15 academy time) records a referral when the registration carrying the code is approved, or when a website inquiry carrying a code is converted to a parent account at registration approval. Only the registration counts as proof, since it is the parent's own signed-in form: anyone who knows a family's email can submit the public inquiry form with their own code, so a referral seen only on an inquiry is recorded as Needs review and never earns by itself. The owner can Approve it in the Referrals card (owner only; the approver and time are kept on the referral), after which it follows the normal hold-then-credit path, crediting at once if the family has already paid past the hold. When a family has both, the registration's code wins over the inquiry's, including when the inquiry was recorded on an earlier day and not yet reviewed. The job marks a referral not eligible when the code is unknown, the code is the family's own (same account, email or phone), or the family already had an invoice before the referral. When the referred family's first invoice (or first monthly tuition invoice, per the trigger) is paid and the hold has passed, the referring family gets the credit (and the new family too, if the owner set one) through the existing account credit ledger, so it comes off their next invoice and stays out of the coach payroll base. Settings > Billing rules gains a read-only Referrals card that lists each referral with its state (needs your review, waiting for the first paid invoice, paid with the credit due date, credited, or not eligible with the reason) and shows the two referral emails as a preview. Nothing is emailed: the copy is preview only and no send path exists yet. New API: `GET /api/v2/parent/referral`, `GET /api/v2/admin/billing/referrals`, `GET /api/v2/admin/billing/referrals/email-preview`, `POST /api/v2/admin/billing/referrals/{referral_id}/approve` (owner only).

## Deploy notes
Migration `0238_referral_program` adds the `referral_codes` collection with `referral_code_parent_unique` (academy, parent) and `referral_code_unique` (academy, code), and the `referrals` collection with `referral_referred_parent_unique` (academy, referred parent: one referral per family), `referral_reward_slot_unique` (academy, reward slot: the yearly cap) and two plain lookup indexes. Partial filters use `$gt: ""`. The collections are new, so it should apply cleanly; if data were already present that would break a unique index, the migration stops and names the collision instead of creating it. The new daily job registers itself in the scheduler and the ops digest staleness check; no env vars.

Owner steps before switching it on:
1. Read and sign off on the email copy (Referrals card > Email copy, source `backend/v2/composition/referral_emails.py`). Sending it is a separate change that needs that sign-off and must stay behind the switch.
2. Set the credits, hold days, yearly cap, expiry and trigger in the Family offers card, then switch the programme on.

## Risk / rollback
With the programme off, nothing runs: the job exits at once, no codes are minted, the parent card is hidden and no referral is recorded. Switching it off stops new referrals and grants at once; credits already granted stay (there is no clawback, by design). Roll back the code by reverting the merge commit; the two collections can stay or be dropped. Each credit is granted exactly once (one credit per referral and role, keyed on the ledger's unique source index), and the yearly cap is held by a unique reward slot per referring family per year, so concurrent or repeated runs, and a repeated Approve, cannot over-grant. Known limits: a referral over the referring family's yearly cap is not eligible as a whole, so the new family gets no credit either; the reward year is the UTC calendar year of the payment; an inquiry-only referral waits for the owner's review indefinitely, and the onboarding form does not yet send a referral code, so until it does most referrals will arrive as Needs review; an inquiry counts only once registration approval links it to a parent account by email; a "household" is matched by shared email or phone, so siblings under different contact details are not caught; credits are written against the parent's user id, so a parent whose invoices use a different linked id may not see the credit applied automatically; the monthly-only trigger treats an invoice with a class and no header source as monthly tuition; the share link uses the address the parent has open, which is the academy page only when the parent app is served on the academy's host.
