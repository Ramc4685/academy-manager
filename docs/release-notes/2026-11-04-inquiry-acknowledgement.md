# inquiry-acknowledgement

PR: #PRNUM

## What changed
Every new website trial request or inquiry now gets one acknowledgement email to the family: the class they chose, whether a seat is available or they are on the waitlist / will be told when classes open, and that the academy will be in touch. It is sent from the same background task as the owner alert (after it), under the academy's display name, with a lead-facing unsubscribe link (`/api/v2/unsubscribe/prospect`) and the CAN-SPAM footer. The form takes any address unverified, so the body carries nothing the submitter typed, one inbox gets at most one acknowledgement per academy per UTC day (`+tag` spellings and Gmail dot / googlemail.com variants count as the same inbox, and the claim is a single insert under a unique index so concurrent submissions send once), an academy sends at most 20 acknowledgements an hour, and replies go only to the academy's configured reply-to (with none, there is no reply line). An address that opts out gets nothing further while the owner is still alerted; a provider failure, an opt-out store outage or a render error gives both the per-contact and the per-inbox claim back so the contact row says no email went out.

## Deploy notes
Migration `0215_email_prospect_opt_outs` adds the unique `(academy_id, email)` index on the new `email_prospect_opt_outs` collection, and migration `0217_crm_inquiry_ack_throttle` adds the unique `(academy_id, mailbox, day)` index and a 7-day TTL on `claimed_at` to the new `crm_inquiry_ack_throttle` collection (both new and empty, no data touched); they apply through the normal `migrate-production` dry-run and `release_command` path, no manual step. No new env vars: the unsubscribe link needs the existing `UNSUBSCRIBE_TOKEN_SECRET` and `FRONTEND_URL`; without them no acknowledgement is sent (unlike digests, there is no account-preferences fallback for a prospect), and the owner alert is unaffected. The link lands on the academy's frontend host and reaches the backend through the existing `/api/v2/*` BFF proxy route. No academy toggle; the acknowledgement is on for every published page with trials open.

## Risk / rollback
If wrong, a family could get a misleading seat/waitlist line or a second email; the claim on the contact row (`acknowledged_at`), the atomic per-inbox daily claim and the honeypot/repeat guards make a double send unlikely, and the opt-out page stops further mail. A released claim is not re-sent by anything yet, so a provider outage during the task means that family hears nothing automatic (the owner alert still lands and the row shows no `acknowledged_at`). Roll back by reverting the PR; the opt-out and throttle collections and the `acknowledged_at` field are harmless left in place (throttle rows expire on their own after 7 days). If the migration has not run, the per-inbox claim still inserts but nothing enforces uniqueness, so the cap is not enforced: the release command must apply 0217 before the acknowledgement is relied on.
