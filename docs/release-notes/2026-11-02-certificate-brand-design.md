## What changed
The level certificate is more colourful and matches the academy's branding: a heavy frame in the academy's brand colour with a volt inner line and shuttle-feather corners, a volt "Certificate of achievement" ribbon, a volt underline under the student's name, volt skill chips, and a level seal in the brand colour with three volt feathers. Volt is only used as a fill or line, never as text on white, so the certificate still reads clearly in print.

## Deploy notes
None. Frontend only. The frame and seal use the academy's Settings brand colour (BLNo should set it to its Ink navy, `#0f172a`, to match the logo).

## Risk / rollback
Visual only. Roll back this PR to return to the plainer certificate; nothing else depends on it.
