# Editing your skill pathway

For academy owners. Your pathway is the list of levels and skills your coaches
mark in class. You edit it in **Settings > Curriculum**: pick the program, and
each level opens with its skills.

## New academies get the revised template

Academies created from now on start with the revised badminton pathway
(decision D-19): split step sits in Level 1, rally scoring, service courts and
faults come in Level 3, and the pathway adds footwork (chassé, six-corner,
rearcourt), the low or high serve choice, serve return, net kill, doubles
rotation and a defensive lift. Two skills have clearer names: "Backhand serve"
is now **Backhand low serve** and "Racket speed" is now **Compact drive
swing**.

Your existing pathway does not change by itself. If you want any of this,
edit it yourself as below.

## Make a skill optional

A level-up needs every **required** skill. A skill most children are still
shaky on can hold a whole class back, so in the template Scissor jump,
Backhand clear, Backhand drop, Round-the-head shot and Jump smash are not
required.

To do the same on your pathway, untick **Required** on the skill's row. The
skill stays on the board and coaches still mark it; a child can simply move up
without it. Tick it again to make it count.

## Add, rename, move or retire a skill

- **Add**: open the level and use **+ Add Skill**. Give it a short name a
  parent would understand and two or three things a coach can see.
- **Rename**: there is no rename button yet. Add the skill under the new name,
  then retire the old one.
- **Move up or down**: use the arrows on the row. Order is what coaches and
  parents see; it does not change who has passed what.
- **Retire**: use **Retire** on the row. The skill leaves the level and
  children who were waiting only on it are queued for a level-up. Past passes
  stay on their record.

To move a skill to another level (for example split step into Level 1), add it
in the new level and retire it in the old one.

## Lesson cards

**Seed lesson cards** in the program editor links the coach lesson cards to
your skills by skill name. A card whose skill you renamed or retired simply
loses that link; it is never attached to a different skill.

## Good to know

- Changes apply straight away for every class on that program.
- Retiring is not deleting: you can always add a skill back.
- Talk to your head coach before making a skill optional. It changes what
  "ready to move up" means for every child.
