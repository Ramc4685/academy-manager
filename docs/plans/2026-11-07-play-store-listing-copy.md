## Full description (4000 chars max)

CourtMastr Academy is the app your sports academy uses to run classes, track
progress and handle billing. Parents, coaches and academy staff sign in with the
account their academy gave them.

For parents
- See your children's classes, upcoming dates and skill level in one place.
- Pay invoices and set up autopay securely with Stripe.
- Report an absence, message coaches and read academy announcements.
- Get a notification when a class date is cancelled, a waitlist seat opens up,
  or a payment is due.

For coaches
- Open today's classes and take attendance, even when the gym Wi-Fi drops.
- Mark skills as players master them and follow the lesson plan for each level.
- Message parents about their player.

For academy staff
- Manage classes, rosters, waitlists and enrollments.
- Send invoices, track payments and keep billing in step with attendance.

CourtMastr Academy is for academies that already use CourtMastr. You need an
account from your academy to sign in.
